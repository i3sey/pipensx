## What's new since 1.6.0

**In short:** large NSZ installs no longer die with "out of memory for piece buffer", 16 MiB piece torrents stream at full AIMD, magnets start warmer, and debrid/NCM spend less time idle.

- No more "out of memory for piece buffer" on big-game installs (#81)
- 16 MiB pieces keep AIMD 8/32/64 instead of freezing at 8
- Large NCM placeholders grow in chunks — no multi-second create stall
- Magnet DHT stays warm; live peer sockets carry into the torrent
- TorBox stream-install pipelines Range requests
- TorrServer fetch overlaps NCM setup
